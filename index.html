💡 Self-Build Guide & Tips
  • Rename Input/Output: Right-click on any switch or probe to edit its name / notation.
  • Connect: Click an output port (circle), then click an input port to wire.
  • Bend Wires: Click anywhere on a wire to add a bend dot. Add N bends and drag them freely.
  • Options: Click any bend dot to open the Circuit & K-Map options menu.
  • Test: Click switches to toggle HIGH (1) / LOW (0) and trace signals live!
